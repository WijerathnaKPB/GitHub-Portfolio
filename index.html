Publications

Smart Alcohol Detection and Engine Locking System

4th Undergraduate Research Symposium 2024 · Nov 22, 2024

Drunk Driving Prevention System: Enhancing Road Safety with Real-Time Intervention

An innovative integration of ICT and engineering designed to combat driving under the influence. This project utilizes MEMS Ethanol Sensors to highly accurately monitor a driver’s Blood Alcohol Concentration (BAC). To prevent impaired driving, the system is designed to automatically activate an engine lock if the BAC exceeds the legal limit of 0.08%, with robust privacy measures and safeguards against false positives.
